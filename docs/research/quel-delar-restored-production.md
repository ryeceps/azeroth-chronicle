# Quel’Delar: The Broken Blade Restored — production record

## Delivery state

Complete 19-node research StoryGuide in the Classic to Wrath StoryTour. Content remains research: secondary quest transcripts and a secondary mirror of an archived Blizzard article support the paraphrases; comparison against the original Wrath 3.3.5a client and editor review remain open. The separate age-of-adventurers StoryGuide is linked only from this story collection. No EraTour or full-history sequence was changed.

## Story boundary and branch map

- Playable trigger: the Battered Hilt is randomized heroic-dungeon loot. No named finder or unique item drop is asserted. The quest begins when acquired, then goes to Quel’Delar’s Rest.
- Faction alternatives: Silver Covenant (Caladis Brightspear) and Sunreavers (Myralion Sunblaze) are separate quest branches. Paired comparative scenes are editorial; they do not put both factions in one in-world event.
- Memory timeline: Alexstrasza’s gift to Anasterian, Anasterian’s trust in Thalorien, and Thalorien’s Third War death precede the Wrath storyline. Lana’Thel’s later destruction at Quel’Delar’s Rest is a separate historical event. The later Thalorien quest test is never narrated as survival or resurrection.
- Restoration: reforging at Pit of Saron; tempering at the Crucible in Forge of Souls; compulsion and Uther’s saronite warning in Halls of Reflection; initial refusal and approach at the reclaimed Sunwell; purification at the Sunwell. Farming loops are not narrated as history.
- Outcome: Alliance and Horde handoffs differ. Aethas’s public presentation belongs to the blood-elf sword branch; class-ineligible sword users can receive another weapon type. No canonical named player is created.
- No map route: map labels are context; theater placements and StoryTour pins are editorial, with unknown geography. No route across Northrend or to Quel’Danas is encoded.

## Research sources and limits

Narration is original paraphrase and contains no quest dialogue. Quest pages and transcripts are evidence locators, not primary evidence. The archived Blizzard Sister Blade article is represented by a secondary mirror that identifies the lost original. Blizzard’s official Icecrown zone guide anchors the Frozen Halls settings. Source, Citation, Claim and Event records are under data/sources, data/citations, data/claims and data/events; all are research status.

Unresolved review: original-client quest sequence, exact 3.3.5a quest variants and reward gates; precise timing of the older flashbacks; original Blizzard article archive; likeness/model resemblance; confirmation that generated Dalaran, Wyrmrest, Pit, Forge, Halls, Quel’Danas and Sunwell scenes match the chosen game edition in client. No quotations, source scans or game assets ship in the app.
