# Master lore backlog — candidate stories for the ten eras

**Status:** editorial research dump; not application input. **Compiled:** 2026-09-29. **Coverage:** Era 0–9. Every item below is `research`, including items supported by Blizzard summaries. No row is a reviewed Claim, an approved date, or permission to render exact geography.

This list extends the existing ten guides. It compares candidate threads with the current guides and event records, so a topic already mentioned in a guide is labeled **deepen** rather than passed off as a new discovery. The first-party links are discovery anchors; claim-level publication still requires exact Chronicle pages, quest/cinematic/raid locations, chronology, independent causal review, and the [citation checklist](citation-review-checklist.md). Book-only leads are marked **verify**. The list is organized by *in-world* era, not by the release date of a game or article.

**Priority:** P0 = current archive is stale or a major missing causal bridge; P1 = strong next story packet; P2 = supporting dossier or a lead needing substantial source work. **Treatment:** new = absent from the current guide; deepen = the guide compresses it; bridge = spans an era boundary and needs an editorial placement decision. Source codes resolve in the source ledger below.

## Long-form story slate — research only

These are candidates for chapter-length guided stories, not approved StoryNodes. A full story needs several consequential scenes, named actors with legible motives, a change in the world, and enough primary material to support its causal links. A collectible item or raid by itself is not enough. The four-beat outlines below are **editorial questions**, not final canon narration; exact quest text, cinematics, and books must be checked before any beat is published. Place chapters by in-world history, with flashbacks labeled when a later quest revisits an older event. Player rewards, server-first titles, repeatable gameplay, and faction-specific viewpoints must not become claims about one canonical hero or one universal experience.
