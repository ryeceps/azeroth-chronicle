# Akama and the Black Temple

**Status:** research; all data records remain `contentStatus: research`  
**Candidate:** `akama-and-black-temple` · **Primary era:** Age of Adventurers  
**Historical range:** Illidan’s earlier seizure of Karabor is brief context; the quest narrative runs through the Burning Crusade Black Temple campaign.  
**Cutoff:** the TBC defeat of Illidan. No later Legion, Cataclysm, or subsequent governance outcome is included.

## Question and thesis

How does Akama sustain a concealed effort to free the Broken while appearing to serve Illidan, and what does that effort cost? The quest sequence presents a gradual answer: the Deathsworn guard a secret; Udalo’s clue and the Heart of Fury clarify the plan; Olum’s capture threatens it; a raid chain makes Akama’s soul the final personal obstacle before the Ashtongue revolt.

The narration follows Akama’s choices and the Broken’s agency. It does not treat each collection quota or raid requirement as a unique historical battle.

## Edition and quest-path audit

- **Historical target:** original *World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade*, patch era 2.0.3–2.4.3; Black Temple and Hyjal raid content arrived in patch 2.1.0.
- **Accessible reconstruction:** Blizzard’s Burning Crusade Classic Phase 3 article publishes the attunement order and raid gates. It is a first-party Classic locator, not capture of original 2007 dialogue or proof that every mechanic was unchanged across TBC patches.
- **Starting variants:** Aldor and Scryer characters receive different opening quest givers. The story explicitly does not combine both openings into one adventurer’s itinerary. The paths converge in the Baa’ri/Ashtongue search.
- **Raid dependencies:** Blizzard’s Classic sequence continues after Akama’s Promise into Serpentshrine Cavern to find Seer Olum after Fathom-Lord Karathress. Ruse of the Ashtongue requires the Tempest Keep/Al’ar task. The Hyjal step requires access to Battle for Mount Hyjal; its separate Vials of Eternity access gate depends on Lady Vashj and Kael’thas. These are game-entry conditions, not causes of Akama’s rebellion.
- **Historical instance:** the Hyjal quest sends the adventurer into a Caverns of Time instance of the Battle of Mount Hyjal. This is shown as a memory and not as present-day travel between Azeroth and Outland.
- **Achievement-title distinction:** the guide never presents “Hand of A’dal” as an in-world rank or office.
- **Unknowns:** exact dates for Akama’s quest events and original-client dialogue/build comparisons remain open. The primary official Phase 3 source covers the Classic reconstruction; secondary quest pages locate the original-era text but are not original-client capture.

## Playable quest dependencies and historical scenes

The Classic source locator orders Tablets of Baa’ri → Oronu the Elder → The Ashtongue Corruptors → The Warden’s Cage → Proof of Allegiance → Akama → Seer Udalo → A Mysterious Portent → The Ata’mal Terrace → Akama’s Promise. Aldor and Scryer openings differ. The post-promise chain continues through The Secret Compromised → Ruse of the Ashtongue → An Artifact From the Past → The Hostage Soul → Entry Into the Black Temple → A Distraction for Akama. The raid story then uses Seek Out the Ashtongue, Redemption of the Ashtongue, and The Fall of the Betrayer.

The guide compresses repetitive collections and detailed raid phases into twenty scenes. It keeps the event that changes the account, the party’s access condition, and the historical consequence distinct.
