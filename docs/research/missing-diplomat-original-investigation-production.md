## Quest dependency outline and narrative join

The original quest chain proceeds through Thomas, Bishop DeLavey, Jorgen, Elling Trias, Watcher Backus, the Duskwood docket, Dashel Stonefist, Tapoke Slim Jahn, Commander Samaul, Archmage Tervosh, Private Hendel and Jaina. Its final playable scene leaves Varian missing. The patch 2.3 continuation begins from Lieutenant Aden’s shipwreck report and proceeds through Captain Wymor, Garn Mathers, Renn McGill, enchanted manacles, Jaina’s Alcaz survey, Bolvar and the return to Jaina. The editorial join groups both threads because they concern the same missing king and Defias operation; it is not asserted to be a playable prerequisite, causal edge, or continuous exact-date sequence.
