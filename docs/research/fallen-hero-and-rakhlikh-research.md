## Historical and playable outlines

### Quest dependency outline

- Alliance opening: Ambassador Ardalan → `Petty Squabbles` → Fallen Hero of the Horde.
- Horde opening: Trebor → `Fall From Grace` → `The Disgraced One` → `The Missing Orders` → Bengor and `The Swamp Talker` → Trebor.
- Shared spine: `A Tale of Sorrow` → `The Stones That Bind Us` → `Heroes of Old` steps → `Kirith` → `The Cover of Darkness` → `The Demon Hunter` → `Loramus` → `Breaking the Ward` → three `The Name of the Beast` steps → `Azsharite` → `The Formation of Felbane` → `Enchanted Azsharite Fel Weaponry` → `Return to the Blasted Lands` → `Uniting the Shattered Amulet` → `You Are Rakh’likh, Demon`.
- `Heroes of Old` is a quest prerequisite/side passage and may yield the Shard of Afrasa. Do not inflate its item handoff into a separate historical event without further evidence.

### Historical scene outline

The first node will state that Classic gives the story two distinct faction introductions. The Alliance approach calls for common action against the Burning Legion; the Horde approach traces missing orders and Bengor’s testimony. The tour then follows the shared historical account: Trebor’s failure and return; the eighteen bound souls and their release; Kirith’s account of the amulet; Loramus’s demon knowledge; the hunt for the true name and ingredients; Felbane’s preparation; the lieutenant amulet fragments; the summoning and defeat of Razelikh; and the horn/ward aftermath. The order of travel is an editorial reading of quest dependencies, not a sourced travel route.
