# Netherwing: Rescue, Disguise, and Liberation

Status: research; no automatic lore approval.

## Candidate and scope

- Candidate ID / slug: `netherwing-liberation`
- Primary era / subperiod: Era 8, The Age of Adventurers / original The Burning Crusade quest era.
- Linked prologue eras: none.
- Historical ending: the exalted confrontation at Dragonmaw Base Camp, Yarzill's flight to Shattrath, and the turn-in to Barthamus. The optional Netherwing drake choice is presented as a player choice, not a universal outcome.
- Excluded later material: Cataclysm and all later Netherwing history, including post-TBC changes to the flight's home and leadership.
- Edition/build: target original TBC quest state, through patch 2.4.3 and before Wrath. Secondary quest transcriptions are used only as locators; original 2.1-era client capture remains open.
- Existing records and overlap: reuses the Era 8 Age of Adventurers, StoryGuide/Storyline/StoryTour playback, worldspace-local story theater, quest-source patterns, the existing Illidan research entity, and Shattrath Lower City location. Does not add scenes to EraTour or retell the Cipher's Netherwing Fields errands.
- Game-area resemblance reference: original TBC Shadowmoon Valley, including the Netherwing Fields, floating Netherwing Ledge, Dragonmaw Fortress/Base Camp, Netherwing Mines, and TBC Shattrath Lower City. The map stages are relational illustrations, not surveyed layouts or routes.

## Historical question and narrative

How does a flight held in bondage turn an outsider's compassion into an alliance, then use that alliance to penetrate the Dragonmaw operation—and what happens when Illidan sees through the borrowed identity?

The continuous spine follows the nine-quest hostile-to-neutral chain; Mordenai's oath and the Dragonmaw disguise; Yarzill's inside help; the optional Murkblood escape-plan investigation; the reputation-gated rise to Exalted; and the final promotion, exposure, rescue, and Shattrath return. The Murkblood branch is an editorially linked one-time investigation, not a prerequisite to the exalted ending. The initial quest sequence does not establish a precise date or route between its named destinations.

## Quest dependencies and variants

1. `Kindness` → `Seek Out Neltharaku` → `Neltharaku's Tale` → `Infiltrating Dragonmaw Fortress` → `To Netherwing Ledge!` → `The Force of Neltharaku` → `Karynaku` → `Zuluhed the Whacked` → `Ally of the Netherwing`.
2. At neutral, `Blood Oath of the Netherwing` → `In Service of the Illidari` → `Enter the Taskmaster`; `Your Friend on the Inside` introduces Yarzill as an ally inside the operation.
3. Daily jobs, gathering, and egg turn-ins repeat and are not separate historical beats. Faction progression is a game gate; elapsed in-world time is unknown.
4. At friendly, `The Great Murkblood Revolt` → `Seeker of Truth` forms an optional one-time testimony/investigation branch. Its dialogue is an attributed Murkblood account. Suspicions about outside buyers are not confirmed findings.
5. At exalted, `Bow to the Highlord` → `Lord Illidan Stormrage` → turn-in to Barthamus. The player is exposed, immobilized, threatened, and rescued by Yarzill. Six possible drake rewards are mutually exclusive; no single choice is imposed by this retelling.

The late faction line has Aldor/Scryer daily variants and profession-dependent tasks outside the authored historical spine. Neither variant is framed as a canonical event sequence. No character's later history is used as proof of a TBC-era claim.
