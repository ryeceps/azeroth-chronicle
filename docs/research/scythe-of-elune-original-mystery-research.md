# The Scythe of Elune: the original mystery — research record

**Status:** research. **Era:** Age of Adventurers (Classic period). **Story ID:** `scythe-of-elune-original-mystery`. **Scope cutoff:** original Classic-era evidence; no Cataclysm, later comic, or Legion-artifact resolution is imported.

## Editorial finding

The Classic trail names a missing priestess, a weapon associated with her prayer, an increasingly uncontrolled worgen summons, and a final search that does not establish the weapon's next bearer. A second Duskwood account by Jitters may concern the same Scythe, but it is a separate quest strand and the consulted text does not prove item identity or a single continuous chain. The story therefore ends with a bounded unknown, not a solved mystery.

This is a narrated editorial route, not an in-world itinerary with verified dates or a demonstrated route. The ninth-quest Scythe chain is preserved in its recorded order. The Jitters testimony appears as a distinct coda and is linked by subject and location only.
