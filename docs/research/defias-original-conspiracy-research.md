They do not establish that Onyxia caused or directed the Defias Brotherhood. The court's unresolved plot is narrated only to the boundary supported by the quest text.

## Central historical question

How does a militia's effort to protect Westfall from an organized brotherhood uncover a former rebuilding guild's grievance, a leader consumed by revenge, and a separate threat within Stormwind's nobility?
