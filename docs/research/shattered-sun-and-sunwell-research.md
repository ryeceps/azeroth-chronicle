# The Shattered Sun and the restored Sunwell

**Status:** research; no lore or art record in this packet is human-approved.  
**Candidate:** 20, class A; Era 8 quest and raid campaign with an Era 5 foundation prologue and a brief Era 7 Sunwell-fall prologue.  
**Cutoff:** original Burning Crusade, patch 2.4 through the Sunwell Plateau epilogue. Exclude all later expansion reinterpretations, including Midnight.

## Question and scope

How did rival peoples become a joint force against Kael'thas's use of the Sunwell, and what did the final defeat and restoration leave for Quel'Thalas? The quest and raid campaign is a linked set of parallel operations, not one adventurer's uninterrupted prerequisite chain. The account follows the Shattered Sun Offensive, its Quel'Danas foothold and Outland support missions, the Magisters' Terrace quest operation, the Sunwell Plateau raid sequence, and the original raid epilogue.

The Sunwell's founding and its corruption during the Third War are short labeled prologues that explain what the campaign is defending. The narrative stops at Velen's original epilogue. It does not import later accounts of Anveena, M'uru, the Sunwell, or the blood elves.

## Chronology and edition

- Place the campaign after the Burning Crusade's patch 2.3 stories and before the Wrath of the Lich King portion of the Classic-to-Wrath StoryTour. The original Sunwell and Magisters' Terrace content is patch 2.4.0 (2008-03-25); exact in-world dates are not assigned.
- Blizzard's official [Timeline Chapter 14: Fury of the Sunwell](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/story/timeline/chapter-14) says “Released in November 2007 | World of Warcraft Patch 2.3” even though its subject is Fury of the Sunwell. This page header conflicts with patch-2.4 evidence and with Blizzard's own later [Burning Crusade Classic Sunwell release article](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-us/news/23789253), which names Fury of the Sunwell as the next content update. Preserve the discrepancy in source notes; do not use the erroneous header as campaign dating.
- The [official Classic retrospective](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/news/23679744/burning-crusade-classic-the-story-so-far) supports the Sunwell's founding and broad earlier history. The official chapter supports Kael'thas's return, betrayal, summoning plan, the joint offensive, the defeat, and Velen's role. It does not cover every named raid participant.
- Patch opening, realm-wide objective completion, available daily quests, gate schedules, raid boss unlocks, raid order, repeated kills, and profession tasks are game systems or player experiences. They must not be narrated as a unique historical order or a single in-world party's itinerary.

## Authored chapter path

The completed 21-scene guide opens with the Sunwell's founding and its corruption during the Third War, then follows Kael'thas's return and plan, the coalition at Shattrath, alternate Aldor/Scryer introductions, the island foothold, phased island objectives, parallel Outland support, and the Magisters' Terrace intelligence and Kael encounters. Its raid path then presents the plateau breach, Kalecgos and Sathrovarr, Brutallus and Madrigosa, Felmyst, the Eredar Twins, M'uru/Entropius, Kil'jaeden, Anveena's intervention, the demon's defeat, the unresolved taint, and Velen and Liadrin's original epilogue. Each node carries its own source, citation and claim records; quest alternatives, server-wide unlocks, parallel operations, encounter order and interpretive composition remain distinct.

No speculative connective tissue makes the campaign a lone player's strict prerequisite path. Where the record supports a source's account rather than a directly observable fact, narration and claim records attribute it. Scene 21, the use of M'uru's heart to renew the Sunwell, closes the story before later expansions.

## Geographic and visual research boundary

Use a dedicated relational story theater, with each MapState and figure composition explicitly labeled as interpretive. It is not an exact dungeon floor plan or proof that depicted people stood together. No route connects Outland with Quel'Danas.

Burning Crusade-era visual anchors for human comparison:

- **Quel'Danas / Sunwell Plateau:** northern Eastern Kingdoms island; sunlit pale limestone and warm gold High Elf ornament; elegant red-gold towers and arcane runes; a damaged and demon-held approach; the Sunwell chamber as a great circular fount with warm gold-white arcane light before the epilogue.
- **Magisters' Terrace:** ordered blood-elf arcane architecture and a bright, polished magical interior on Quel'Danas; keep it distinct from the darker raid chamber and from modern/rebuilt versions.
- **Shattrath / Terrace of Light:** open draenei stonework and the suspended, luminous naaru presence; joint staging should read as draenei and blood-elf cooperation, not one faction's city taking over.
- **Outland support:** retain the broken red-violet sky, floating landforms, fractured terrain and patch-appropriate regional environments. Use a representative relational montage rather than claiming a route between every daily mission.

All generated art is original interpretation. The visual ledger will record per-asset edition, recognizable materials, landform and color cues, figure/object roster, source locator, reuse decision, and the unresolved client-comparison result. As in the project template, place-based resemblance review remains open until compared against the intended original TBC client.
