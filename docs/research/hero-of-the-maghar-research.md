# Hero of the Mag'har: Garrosh, Geyah, and Thrall's Return

Status: research. Candidate 17 in the through-Wrath slate, after the Scepter of the Shifting Sands. Primary era: Era 8 / The Burning Crusade, Horde quest chain added in patch 2.0.3. Historical ending: the original chain through **Thrall, Son of Durotan** and **Hero of the Mag'har**. Later Garrosh history is outside the cutoff.

## Scope and question

The story asks how the account of Grom's final choice changes what his son believes about their name. It follows the Horde-only quest sequence from Garadar through the ancestor inquiry, Shattrath and Auchindoun, Geyah's message to Thrall, and the closing visit in Nagrand. The Third War at Demon Fall Canyon is shown only as a labeled memory within Thrall's telling. This is a chronological arrangement of quest evidence, not a claim that one player character experienced every prerequisite.

The user-facing collection is the independent **Classic-to-Wrath StoryTour**. The guide remains Era 8 research content for browsing and direct playback; it is not added to EraTour. The 15-node continuous sequence compresses gathering/repetition and joins the two adjacent ancestor-investigation beats while retaining both quest sources.

## Edition, variants, and historical boundary

- Game edition: original *The Burning Crusade*, Horde chain, introduced in patch 2.0.3. The source locators available during authoring reproduce quest records; they are not claimed as original-client captures.
- The original TBC ending continues beyond **There Is No Hope** through the message to Thrall and his return to Garadar. Those continuation quests were removed in patch 4.0.1, so a current-client experience may stop earlier.
- Exact in-world dates, duration of cross-world travel, the precise ordering among unrelated prerequisites, and an original-client/build identifier remain unknown.
- Required prerequisite branches are recorded as alternatives in the story review note: the Totem of Kar'dash/Murkblood branch and Nagrand diplomacy/Forge Camp branches ending at **Message to Garadar** and **Forge Camp: Annihilated**. The story does not say that every Nagrand quest is required.
- No later wars, Warchief decisions, or Garrosh outcomes are foreshadowed.

## Existing records and overlap

The story reuses existing Thrall, Grom Hellscream, Mannoroth, A'dal, Shattrath, and Orgrimmar records where identity matches. New story-specific records cover Garrosh, Geyah, Mother Kashur, K'ure, D'ore, the Mag'har ensemble, the ancestral theater, and its scene states. The pre-existing Era 8 guide and the separate Karazhan story remain independent. The Third War memory references the existing Grom event and Third War research without turning either into present-day Nagrand geography.

## Visual references and open questions

The illustrated Nagrand scenes use the TBC area reference gallery for its green-gold grass, turquoise waterways, broad-canopy trees, flat mesas, floating islands, waterfalls, and Oshu'gun's pale mass. Garadar is a modest hide-and-timber settlement, not an imposing stone city. The Auchenai scene uses the crypt's blue-gray Draenei arches and restrained violet-blue light. The Demon Fall Canyon artwork is an interpretive memory scene with barren Ashenvale rock and a distinct memory label. Shattrath and Orgrimmar images are reused from TBC stories and remain broad contextual scenes. No image claims to reproduce an exact quest camera, surveyed route, or dungeon floor plan.
