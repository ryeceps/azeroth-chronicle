# Drakuru: Trust, Betrayal, and Infiltration — research and production ledger

Status: complete illustrated research story; not reviewed or published. 20 scenes; 1535 narration words. Era 8 / Wrath of the Lich King. The guide begins with Drakuru already caged at Granite Springs and ends with the Lich King killing him at Voltarus. It does not claim an exact date or order relative to Wrathgate.

## Source and edition boundary

Quest order and dialogue leads come from secondary Warcraft Wiki pages reproducing original quest text and event scripts. They are not original 3.3.5a client captures. Blizzard’s Wrath Classic zone guides support area identity and visual traits only. Original quest text, faction/return branches, versioned area art, named models, and final-script capture must be checked before promotion. Narration is an original paraphrase; game instructions, item-use mechanics, drop counts, and combat rotations are not narrated as history.

## Story spine

1. Drakuru proposes a blood pact while held at Granite Springs.
2. The Vial of Visions carries his image through a search for artifacts, whose supposed protective history remains his claim.
3. The ruby Eye, Heart and Drakkari tablets lead to the summit of Drak’Tharon.
4. The Lich King exposes Drakuru’s betrayal, empowers him and assigns Zul’Drak.
5. Orders reveal the Scourge plan; Stefan Vadu and the Ebon Blade begin a separate counter-operation.
6. The returning chain and faction introduction vary; the infiltration then exposes the blight army.
7. Drakuru’s own Scepter of Domination turns the Blightblood Trolls against him. The Lich King kills him and spares the infiltrator.

The Grizzly Hills and Zul’Drak sections are linked by the quest chain, but the story does not assert exact relative timing against the separate Wrathgate campaign. “Reunited” and “Dark Horizon” preserve prior-chain differences. Alliance and Horde infiltration approaches remain variant rather than one canonical traveler.
