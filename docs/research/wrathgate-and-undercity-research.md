# The Wrathgate and Undercity

**Status:** research; no lore, chronology, or art record in this packet is human-approved.
**Candidate:** 21, class A; a 19-node Wrath of the Lich King campaign story.
**Cutoff:** the Wrathgate disaster and the Wrath-era Battle for Undercity. Excludes later knowledge about Bolvar and the Fourth War Battle for Lordaeron.

## Question and scope

How did the Alliance and Horde converge on Angrathar, and how did separate accounts of the disaster lead both factions back to Undercity? This story follows the Alliance and Horde approaches as attributed parallel accounts, the fall of the gate, the two distinct city operations, and the confrontation that Jaina ended by withdrawing the Alliance. The interleaved order is an editorial reading sequence, not evidence that the two assaults occurred at the same time.

The guide is linked to placard 21 on the separate Classic-to-Wrath map. It sits after the Burning Crusade patch-2.4 Sunwell story and before Quel’Delar. This is standalone StoryTour content, not an EraTour insertion.

## Authored chapter path

1. **The road to Angrathar** — separate Alliance and Horde approaches; the two armies gather against the Scourge.
2. **The fall of the gate** — Dranosh falls; plague attacks the lines; the Red Dragonflight cleanses the field; Stormwind receives an early report.
3. **Two paths beneath Lordaeron** — the coup's aftermath; the Horde's return, Jaina's warning, the Alliance descent, the Horde entry, the broken lift, and Varimathras's defeat; then the Alliance reaches the Apothecarium and Putress.
4. **A war barely averted** — the leaders converge at the throne; Jaina withdraws the Alliance; the northern war continues under unresolved mistrust.

The two Undercity operations are interleaved for a readable continuous guide. Neither their close succession in the narration nor the StoryTour placard order claims exact synchronization. Each node has an authored environment MapState, transcript, and source-linked claim; the full sequence remains research.
