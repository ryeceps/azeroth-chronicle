# Questline story candidates — through Wrath of the Lich King

**Status:** research selection, not approved lore or finished stories. **Compiled:** 2026-09-30. **Scope:** original World of Warcraft, The Burning Crusade, and Wrath of the Lich King, ending with Wrath's released epilogue content. Exclude the Cataclysm prelaunch world changes and every later expansion. Earlier history is welcome as a sourced prologue; later character developments are outside this slate.

This is the focused questline selection within the broader [master lore backlog](master-lore-backlog.md). That older archive remains broader in time; it is not the build queue for this request. Use the [storyline build template](storyline-build-template.md) for every candidate here. These 30 candidates are an editorial selection, not a quota or an assertion that each matches the Scepter's scale.

## Selection and source rules

- **A — build first:** strong narrative with travel across zones, worlds, or multiple instances. The Scepter is the benchmark.
- **B — substantial regional story:** enough characters, reversals, and consequences for a full telling, but less geographic reach.
- **C — research before scheduling:** compelling material with an incomplete ending, linked rather than continuous chains, or a larger source burden.
- Geography below is a research itinerary, not a verified route or declaration of current dungeon requirements. Distinguish quest destinations, historical scenes, and proposed raid aftermath in the packet.
- Named quests are primary evidence to capture in the identified game version. Wiki links are **secondary discovery locators**, including when they reproduce game text. Search results confirmed these leads; some pages refused full retrieval. No complete quest transcript audit has been performed. Capture quest IDs, original text, dialogue, cinematics, faction variants, and edition/build before authoring claims.
- Official articles establish only the facts they actually state. Their modern Classic release schedules and access rules do not establish the original historical sequence. Rewards, repeated kills, daily resets, reputation thresholds, and server unlock times are gameplay, not additional story events.

**Recommended first production wave:** Scepter → Onyxia → Dungeon Set 2 → Karazhan → Akama/Black Temple → Quel'Delar. Keep separate packets for overlapping stories and cite the shared events once. All main quest journeys belong in **Era 8** in this repository; Classic, Burning Crusade, and Wrath should be visible subperiod labels. Ancient and Third War prologues can link to earlier eras without moving the later playable questline there.
