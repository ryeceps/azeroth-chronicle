# Swift Flight Form: the raven's legacy

Status: research; source, area-art, item, audio and original-client review remain open.
Candidate: `swift-flight-form-raven-legacy` (questline candidate 18)
Primary era: Age of Adventurers; Burning Crusade, patch 2.1 quest chain. This account stops before the Wrath 3.0.3 trainer change and Cataclysm 4.0.1 removal.
Game edition/build: quest text is located through the Warcraft Wiki compilation and the current Burning Crusade Classic Anniversary edition. No original 2.1–2.4.3 client transcript was captured for this packet. Race/faction variants: Druid class chain, available to eligible night elf and tauren Druids during the original TBC era; use no single canonical player character.
Historical ending: Morthis awards the knowledge of Swift Flight Form; his final quest leaves Anzu's return a continuing concern. Do not claim the threat is eliminated forever.
Excluded: later Emerald Dream, Dreamgrove, Legion class-campaign, Firelands, Warlords, and subsequent Druid campaign lore; later changes to how the form is trained; encounter tactics, mount drops, herb quotas, and repeatable gameplay as historical events.

## Question and evidence boundary

How do the Druids of the Talon investigate a disturbance reported inside the Emerald Dream, recover the raven cult's history, and use that knowledge to challenge Anzu? The quest order documents a class-specific trial and a path of evidence. Quest dialogue presents Clintar's belief about an outside presence, then Arthorn's research and the Book of the Raven identify Anzu and describe an older cult account. The early claim that Anzu is already responsible for the disturbance is therefore presented as the characters' working suspicion, not an independently verified narrator fact.

The chain is a narrative quest sequence, not evidence of exact travel duration, historical dates, one named player's experience, or a fully understood ancient history. The three bird essences and the Moonstone are prerequisites for the Sethekk Halls challenge, not a general origin story for the Emerald Dream. Repeated herb, stone, and combat objectives are compressed while their discoveries and separate named sites remain visible.

## Visual target and human review

Use the original Burning Crusade 2.0.3–2.4.3 look as reference where it exists: Zangarmarsh's flooded teal marsh, spore mounds and oversized mushroom caps; Moonglade's moonlit conifer grove and night-elf druidic buildings; Blade's Edge's jagged ochre peaks, broken bridges and floating crags; the distinctive wooded Evergrove; the crystal-spired Vortex Pinnacle; Terokkar's dry pale forest, Skettis's arakkoa architecture and spire-filled skyline; and Auchindoun's circular pale stone complex, with Sethekk's darker avian interior. Nagrand must retain open green pasture, mesas, shallow water and floating islands. Azshara/Southfury should retain its original pre-Cataclysm forest, river and eastern cliff character. Do not render modern post-Cataclysm geography as if it were the TBC map.

These are area-language briefs, not survey drawings. No exact dungeon floor plan or unsourced actor formation is claimed. Keep the Dream visibly interpretive. The Stormcrow form is treated as a quest name, not as a separate character race. Generated scene and cast art is listed per node, with source prompt file, converted-image hash, and visual review status in [the visual asset ledger](swift-flight-form-raven-legacy-visual-assets.json). TBC screenshot galleries are visual locators only. A human must compare every reused or generated area illustration and actor/model with the corresponding 2.0.3–2.4.3 client/build. The underlying quest text and exact prerequisite edges also need primary client capture. Content stays at `research` until those reviews.
