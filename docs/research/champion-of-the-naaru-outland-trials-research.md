# Champion of the Naaru: Trials across Outland

Status: research.

## Candidate and scope

- Candidate ID / slug: `champion-of-the-naaru-outland-trials`.
- Product work: era-story content in the existing Storyline → StoryGuide → StoryTour flow; no EraTour change and no engine change.
- Edition: original Burning Crusade quest era, with 2.0.3–2.4.3 as the bounded research window. Wrath-era and later changes are excluded except where a source is used to explain a version boundary.
- Starting point: the completed Cipher of Damnation quest. This story begins with the Khadgar handoff and does not retell Oronok’s chain.
- Ending point: A’dal’s final Trial of the Naaru: Magtheridon and the Tempest Key / access to The Eye. The separate Cudgel of Kar’desh chain is only named to bound the `Champion of the Naaru` title condition; it is not merged into this narrative.
- Cast is limited to A’dal, Khadgar, the unnamed prisoner group, Kargath Bladefist, Warlord Kalithresh, Murmur, Millhouse Manastorm, Magtheridon, and a non-specific adventurer company. No unsupported roster or faction is inferred.

The first three trials are independent requirements, not a chain of events that must happen in a single historical order. The guide presents them in Blizzard's attunement-overview order for readability. The Strength quest itself defines two named objectives; it is treated as one divided trial with two scenes, not as two trials. The title's eligibility is patch-sensitive and requires a separate Serpentshrine access path in Blizzard's retrospective guide; the four trials alone are not narrated as an in-world appointment or universal title award.

## Historical question and evidence

**Question:** How does A’dal answer the threat revealed by the Cipher of Damnation, and what do the four trials actually test or open?

The TBC quest descriptions and completion text for quest IDs 10883, 10884, 10885, 10886, and 10888 were reviewed in full through the linked quest-text transcriptions. These are secondary reproductions of primary in-game text, not an original-client capture. Each record and citation labels that limit. Narration will paraphrase the quest text; no dialogue is copied into public prose.

Blizzard’s attunement overview supplies the sequence from the Cipher to Khadgar’s letter and A’dal, lists the four trials and their heroic/raid destinations, and distinguishes The Eye access from the separate Serpentshrine Cavern chain used for the title. The 2022 Classic hotfix is used only to document the later Classic access state; it is not evidence for original TBC chronology.

## Event outline and proposed nodes

1. **A letter after the Cipher** — Khadgar’s missive sends the completed Shadowmoon investigation to Shattrath; no second account of Oronok’s discoveries.
2. **A’dal’s warning** — the quest text links Kael’thas and the Cipher to a danger to what remains of Outland; the threat and A’dal’s stated purpose are attributed.
3. **Mercy in the Shattered Halls** — A’dal’s account names three prisoners held by Kargath and requires their rescue; the “unused” axe is proof of the deed, not an independent historical artifact.
4. **The divided trial of Strength: Steamvault** — Kalithresh’s defeat and trident, as one half of the quest’s explicitly divided trial.
5. **The divided trial of Strength: Shadow Labyrinth** — Murmur’s defeat and essence, the other named half. The scene pairing is editorial; it does not add a motive or causal link between the two targets.
6. **Tenacity in the Arcatraz** — A’dal attributes Millhouse’s presence to a stowaway caught by circumstance and asks that he be rescued and survive. That explanation remains attributed to A’dal.
7. **The final trial** — A’dal calls for an army and the defeat of Magtheridon deep inside Hellfire Citadel.
8. **The Tempest Key** — the final quest completion grants The Tempest Key and the original access route to The Eye.
9. **A title beyond one key** — a bounded epilogue separates the Eye key from the distinct Serpentshrine access chain; it labels the title as a patch-era reward rather than an in-world rank or universal outcome.

Nine nodes, with one node per required trial and a paired two-scene treatment for Strength. The first three trials are parallel requirements; their display order is editorial. Target spoken runtime is approximately 5–7 minutes after transcript stabilization. No farming, group composition, dungeon timer, reputation, loot or key-purchasing steps are padded into the narrative. Final authored nodes: 1. A letter after the Cipher; 2. The danger named by A’dal; 3. Mercy in the Shattered Halls; 4. Strength, divided: the Steamvault; 5. Strength, divided: Murmur; 6. Tenacity in the Arcatraz; 7. Only Magtheridon remains; 8.
