# Shadowmourne and the relics of the fallen king

Status: research packet; not human-reviewed. Candidate #24 in the Classic-to-Wrath questline slate.

## Scope and production decision

- **Candidate ID:** `shadowmourne-and-arthas-relics`
- **Primary era:** Era 8, the Age of Adventurers; Wrath of the Lich King, patch 3.3.0 through 3.3.3 quest additions.
- **Historical ending:** the Lich King's defeat at the Frozen Throne and the five original personal-relic returns introduced in patch 3.3.3. Excludes all later Shadowlands explanations or outcomes.
- **Game reference:** Wrath of the Lich King, target client/build 3.3.5a. Quest pages are secondary text mirrors, not verified client captures.
- **Central question:** can the living shape an answer to Frostmourne without repeating the harm it made?
- **Editorial boundary:** Shadowmourne is pursued by Death Knights, Paladins and Warriors. The guide uses one illustrated death-knight viewpoint solely as an interpretive example; no named or canonical adventurer is asserted. The other class variants remain in the narration and review notes.
- **Standalone delivery:** independent Classic-to-Wrath StoryTour entry after Quel'Delar; never inserted into the Era 8 tour or full-history tour.

## Quest dependency and editorial order

`The Sacred and the Corrupt` → `Shadow's Edge` → `A Feast of Souls` → Unholy Infusion → Blood Infusion → Frost Infusion → `The Splintered Throne` → Empowerment → `Shadowmourne...` → `The Lich King's Last Stand` → Sealed Chest / `Personal Property` → five parallel relic returns.

The first quest returns from Icecrown Citadel to Frostmourne Cavern in Dragonblight. The three infusions are ordered by the documented chain; the guide does not infer that their raid mechanics happened as one combat encounter. The five chest branches are parallel follow-ups, not five mandatory events in one travel itinerary. A single editorial playlist order is used for readability and does not claim exact relative timing among the returns.

## Proposed playable scenes

1. Darion puts forward an audacious weapon against Frostmourne.
2. At Frostmourne Cavern, the former hammer of Arthas is recovered under the Lich King's opposition.
3. At Light's Hammer, sacred and corrupt components form Shadow's Edge.
4. Repeated souls strengthen the unfinished weapon; repeated collection is compressed.
5. Professor Putricide's power is turned against his own design.
6. Lana'thel's blood marks the second infusion.
7. Sindragosa guards the last infusion in Frostwing Halls.
8. Frozen Throne shards bind the axe to the Lich King's seat of power.
9. Darion completes its empowerment.
10. Shadowmourne is presented as complete, while its resemblance to Frostmourne leaves its danger unresolved.
11. At the Frozen Throne, Tirion breaks Frostmourne and the Lich King falls; do not claim the axe alone caused the victory.
12. A sealed chest reveals five keepsakes of Arthas's life.
13. Sylvanas receives the vial of her blood.
14. Uther receives Arthas's Silver Hand badge and chooses the memory of the eager young defender.
15. Muradin receives the blunt training sword.
16. Jaina receives the locket she once gave Arthas.
17. Darion returns Alexandros's soul shard; father and son are reunited.

Starting production length: 17 nodes, grouped into four chapters. The three infusions and five separate relic voices justify the length; material collection and repeated encounters do not.
