# Netherwing liberation — production ledger

Status: research StoryGuide; lore and visual review remain open. Target: original World of Warcraft: The Burning Crusade quest state before Wrath. Narration is paraphrased from secondary quest locators. The exact original 2.1–2.4.3 client comparison is not claimed.

## Evidence and source handling

Each scene below has its own event, active claim, exact citation record and source. The cited quest/wiki pages are secondary transcriptions and sequence locators, not captured original-client evidence. In-story allegations stay attributed to the Murkblood speaker; the six Netherwing drakes remain a player choice. Repeated reputation tasks are summarized as an undated game-system interval rather than separate historical episodes.
